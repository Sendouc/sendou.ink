Language changes

- Weapon, gear, stage and mode names can be shown in a different language than the rest of the site. Pick the one your game is in from the Locale settings
- In-game names are available in every language Splatoon 3 is: also in Italian, Korean, Dutch, Russian and Canadian French
- The site itself is now available in English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese and Chinese. Danish, Hebrew, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Italian, Korean, Russian and Canadian French translations were removed as they were not kept up to date
- Spanish and French speakers are shown the variant of their region by default
